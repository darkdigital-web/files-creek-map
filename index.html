Water here: Right Fork Files Creek

Google Maps labels the stream Right Fork Files Creek, but the host says that label is in the wrong place. The real water runs through the wooded tree line just east of the pond, and it's the creek you drive over on the Files Creek Bridge as you come in. On the drawn map, Files Creek Trail follows it.

From the property tour
  • Getting there: search "The Trails at Files Creek" in Google Maps and it takes you to the front gate. The back way is hillier. The host offered to meet Tuesday and lead the convoy in.
  • Name mix-up: Google says "Back Rd", but the host calls it Files Creek Road. Same road.
  • Private roads: everything inside the gate is private, and each cabin has its own address.
  • Uphill = left: on the drawn map, moving left (up Switchback Rd) means climbing. The creek is at the bottom of the mountain and Pine Ridge is near the top, about 430 ft higher.
  • Your cabins: Mountain Bench (the bottom cabin, about halfway along Little Stream Trail, in the trees) and Pine Ridge (on the ridge, = 658 Switchback Rd). Both are blocked and booked.
  • Pine Ridge: surrounded by 40-year-old pines, planted as Christmas trees and never harvested. They're the dark-green patch in the satellite view.
  • Park Forest Trail: the very top, far left of the drawn map. The host's pick for tent camping and a fire, next to an open field.
  • Getting around: side-by-sides are available, including the host's own.
  • Locations: both open-field and wooded options. Fields read green or managed, so on camera they need a story reason (a bear sign, gunshots) or should stay out of frame.
  • Lunch: the host can ask the local Mexican restaurant to deliver both days. They've never been up to the property, so directions TBD.

At a glance

The water
The creek runs under the Files Creek Bridge, the bridge you drive over to come in. Files Creek Trail follows it along the bottom of the property. Google's "Right Fork Files Creek" label sits slightly off: look for the wooded tree line just east of the pond.
The pond
In the open valley meadow right after the front gate. Your landmark for finding the creek.
Getting in
Search Google Maps for "The Trails at Files Creek" to reach the front gate, off Files Creek Rd (Google calls it "Back Rd"). All roads inside are private.
The climb
Switchback Rd climbs west about 430 ft: creek ~2,485 ft → Pine Ridge ~2,913 ft → Park Forest ~2,916 ft. On the drawn map, left = uphill. Side-by-sides available.
Your cabins
Mountain Bench (bottom cabin, in the trees near the valley, location approximate) and Pine Ridge (658 Switchback Rd, ringed by 40-year-old pines).
Camp & fire
Park Forest Trail, at the very top next to an open field.
